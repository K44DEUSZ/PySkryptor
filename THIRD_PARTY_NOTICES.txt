PySkryptor Third-Party Notices
=============================

This is the component guide for the modernized source tree.
Each assembled build also contains licenses/gui, licenses/cpu and, when
included, licenses/cuda. Their inventory.json files identify distributions
detected in the corresponding PyInstaller analysis. Full license and NOTICE
files supplied by those distributions are preserved alongside the inventories.
An inventory is evidence of build contents, not approval of redistribution.

Release review
--------------

Before public distribution, verify native libraries and Qt plugins, source
availability obligations, all required copyright/NOTICE texts, and model terms
when weights are offered separately. Review downloader behavior and the terms
of each supported service independently of the software licenses.
